Set something that will be read on a page: a paper, a book, a poster, a manual, a curriculum, a report, a catalogue, a legal filing.

It will not take an impression on a broken forme. Eight checks stand between a folder of plates and a permanent record, and each exists because that defect reached one.

The workflow

What it asks you, in order.

These questions are this room's own. The cycling mechanism is shared with the other rooms; the questions are not, because the work is not.

  1. platesWhat is on each plate?
  2. placeWhich part does it belong to, and in what order?
  3. leadWhat does a reader meet before they see it?
  4. lookWhat should they look at first, and why is it load-bearing?
  5. indexHow would a contents list name it? What are its index terms?
  6. abstractWhat does the whole work contain?

Which AI

Yours. Or none.

The cycler is not an AI provider. It puts a question on your clipboard and takes an answer back. Links below come from ai.config.json — edit it, add to it, delete from it. The list is alphabetical, not a ranking, and a server on your own machine or your own typing works exactly as well.

What it must know first

  • the plates themselves
  • which plate is the jacket
  • the parts a reader moves through
  • who is publishing it and under what licence

What it holds between steps

  • plate table
  • part structure
  • cover selection
  • the abstract

What comes out

  • A kitab-shaped book: parts, chapters, figures that talk, a sealed manifest and a DOI.

What must never stop being true

  • a plate is never minted twice under two names
  • the jacket is never counted as a chapter
  • no file reaches the deposit that no chapter declares

How it goes wrong

  • duplicate bytes under different filenames
  • template placeholders riding along
  • a stale title inherited from the book it was derived from
  • a blank deposit abstract

What it needs before it will publish

  • a content hash per plate
  • a sealed manifest
  • a timestamp before the mint

On your own machine.
